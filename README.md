# THE WORLD TODAY — Monday 23 November 2026

One image summing up the world's news of Monday 23 November 2026: the AI rout
that hit Asia (Kospi -3.3%), Guterres calling for global AI rules at Bridgetown,
and the US lead over China at a record low (Asia Power Index gap 5.3) — plus
28 more stories across six regions, a world map with numbered pins, and a
DATE CHECK box that keeps out anything that did not happen on 23 November.

`draw.py` (Pillow, stdlib + PIL) draws `entry.png` (1024×1024). `main.py`
copies it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` and verifies size and sha256.

Scenes are drawn illustrations, not photographs.
